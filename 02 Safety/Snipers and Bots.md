# Snipers and Bots
- Bots watch launches and buy within **milliseconds** using priority fees / Jito bundles / private RPC.
- Humans are structurally late. 93 of top-100 Pump.fun wallets by activity were bots (Dune, June 2025).
- Pisa study (655,770 tokens, Sep-Oct 2025): bot-active tokens had *lower* graduation probability after the early phase; **large coordinated human buys** predicted graduation best. So bots != the "pump engine".

## Implications
- Don't try to out-speed. Don't buy in the first seconds manually.
- Look at what's *left* after snipers: distribution, holders, who's still holding. See [[Spotting Winners]].
- Sniper supply is smeared selling; [[Bundling]] is coordinated.

## Counter-tactics creators use (know the game)
- Self-bundling tools exist openly (GitHub "bundlers", "sniper dump" modes). That's literally manufactured supply control. Be skeptical of any launch that looks too clean.
