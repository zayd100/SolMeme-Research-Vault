# Pre-Trade Checklist
Mnemonic: **CASH-E**

- **C**hart/curve: age, momentum, liquidity vs market cap. Did it already 5x? -> [[Not Chasing Big Candles]]
- **A**uthority & bundle: mint/freeze authority renounced? bundled %? ([[Bundle Detection Checklist]])
- **S**upply spread: top-10 holders, snipers, dev wallet, cluster map
- **H**ype source: who is talking? organic or paid? early or late? ([[Attention Economy and CT]])
- **E**xit plan written *before* entry: size, ladder, invalidation. ([[Exit Strategy and Scaling Out]])

## Then
- Size by [[Position Sizing and Bankroll]] (fixed small unit)
- Log entry reason in [[Trade Journal]] (one sentence)

## Hard "no" list
- No bundle check done
- Chasing after a vertical candle
- Not sure how to sell
- Using money you can't lose
- Tilted/tired ([[Psychology and Tilt]])
