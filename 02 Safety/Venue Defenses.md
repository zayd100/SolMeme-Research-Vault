# Venue Defenses (launch mechanics vs bundling/sniping)
From Meme Central guide (~Sept 2026) - *medium confidence*:

- **Pump.fun**: bonding curve; at graduation LP migrates **atomically to PumpSwap** (no migration snipe).
- **Pons.family** (Robinhood Chain): **no bonding curve, no migration**; fixed supply; pool live from block one. -> nothing to snipe at migration, but launch-block competition remains.
- **hood.fun**: bonding curve then auto-migration to **locked Uniswap v3** pool.
- **Pools.trade** (Uniswap Labs, launched **5 Aug 2026**): "Crowd Launch" mode designed against bundling.

## Takeaways
- Different venues = different attack surfaces. Learn the mechanic before trading it.
- "Anti-bundle" marketing != guarantee. Still run [[Bundle Detection Checklist]].
