# Rug Pull Patterns
Mnemonic: **"DUMP"**
- **D**ev sells / dev wallets linked to top holders
- **U**nnatural clusters (funded-together wallets)
- **M**ass same-slot buys ([[Bundling]])
- **P**ush-only hype (paid/KOL posts, no organic community)

## Other patterns
- **Copycat of a trending name** (fake CA of a real hype coin) - verify CA from the project's *official* account
- **Honeypot** (can't sell) - test with tiny size/sell simulation in your tool
- **Fake partnerships/listings/burns** announced to pump price
- **Slow rug**: dev drips sells through many wallets
- **Serial deployer**: same wallet launching dozens of tokens. Check deployer history.
- **Insider wallets** pre-funded then buying right after launch

## Rules
- Never buy off a *screenshot* CA. Copy from source.
- If you can't explain why you're early, you're probably exit liquidity.
