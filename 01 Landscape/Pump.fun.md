# Pump.fun
Permissionless Solana launchpad (launched Jan 2024). Anyone makes a token in seconds; trades on a **bonding curve**, then "graduates" to **PumpSwap** (its own AMM, launched Mar 2025).

## Key facts (with confidence)
- ~40,000 launches/day reported Jan 2026 (aixbt) - *medium*
- Revenue: ~$610M in 2025 (reported); **~$51.0M over 30 days to 22 Sep 2026** (Upshift) - *medium*
- Graduation threshold quoted as **~85 SOL** in one guide - *verify, may change*
- LP migrates atomically to PumpSwap at graduation (removes front-running of the migration itself) - [[Venue Defenses]]
- Mar 2026: platform **locked creator-fee redirects** to curb abuse (crypto.news headline)
- Acquired Padre -> now "Terminal" trading terminal
- 93 of the top 100 wallets by activity were **bots** (Dune, June 2025) - see [[Snipers and Bots]]
- Jan 2026 BiP hackathon: $250k funding x12 projects (builder angle - see [[Content Ideas Bank]])

## Who wins here
- Platform: always (fees on every trade). See [[Who Actually Makes Money]].
- Bots/snipers: structural speed edge.
- Manual traders: mostly small outcomes. See [[Data Reality Check]].
