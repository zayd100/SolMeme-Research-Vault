# Robinhood Chain and Pons
- **Mainnet: 1 July 2026.** Built for tokenized real-world assets (RWAs); memes took most DEX volume anyway.
- **Record DEX volume $874.8M on 30 Aug 2026** (Dune via press); other writeups cite ~$1.5-2B/day around early Sept - sources disagree, *medium*.
- **Pons** = main launchpad. ~59% of launchpad volume on 1 Sep (~$370M of $623M). >$4.5B cumulative in <2 months (Memeburn).
- Top traded on 30 Aug: PONS (~$67M vol), **Cash Cat** (~$33M).
- Pons design: no bonding curve, fixed supply, pool live from block one. See [[Venue Defenses]].

## The twist
Memecoins paired with **Robinhood Stock Tokens** instead of SOL/USDC. See [[Stock-Paired Memecoins]].

## How to treat it
- Real volume, but rotating memecoin capital. Sustainability unknown.
- Grok's "~8% of Solana meme volume" - **not verified**. See [[Grok Brief - Fact Check]].
- Different chain = different tools, different scams. Re-learn [[Tools and Trackers]] and [[Wallet Security and Scams]] before touching it.
- Listing in the Robinhood app is **not** endorsement.
