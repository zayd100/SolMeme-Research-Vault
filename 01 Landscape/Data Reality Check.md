# Data Reality Check - "do traders win?"
**Same platform, conflicting headlines. Method matters.**

| Claim | Source | Period |
|---|---|---|
| ~49-50.6% of ~1.4M wallets lost money; ~45% made <$500; **~96% lost or made <$500** | Dune dashboard by @oladee (via crypto.news, CMC) | Mar 2026 |
| **73.3% of wallets had positive realized PnL**; 65.1% of those made $1-$500; only **5.4% made >$1,000** | CoinGecko Research / Dune | Apr 2026 |
| Profitable share low of **30.1%** | CoinGecko | Jun 2025 |
| "97% of traders lose money" | aixbt (X) | Jan 2026 |
| 93 of top 100 wallets = bots | Adam_Tehc (Dune) | Jun 2025 |
| 98.5% of Pump.fun tokens dumped before Raydium | CryptoSlate via Substack | older (2024-25) |

## Why they disagree
- Different months, definitions (realized vs unrealized, fees, tiny dust wallets, multi-wallet users, bots)
- CoinGecko's hypothesis: losers **left** (monthly active wallets 5.2M May 2025 -> 1.8M Dec 2025), leaving a tougher field.
- "Profitable" can mean +$3. Profit size matters more than win rate.

## Honest read
- Most people make little. A thin top tail makes the real money (2 wallets >$1M in Mar 2026 per the dashboard).
- Grok's "20-40% win rate" is plausible per-trade, but not directly backed by these wallet-level stats.

## Use
- Plan for **being average = small win or small loss**. Your edge is avoiding big losses. See [[Preserving Capital]].
