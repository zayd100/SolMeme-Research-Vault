# FOMO App (fomo.family)
Social-first mobile trading app by FOMO Labs (SF team, ex-dYdX). Launched **6 May 2025** as Solana-only.

## What it is
- One USD balance; fund with **Apple Pay / card**; no bridging/gas hassle
- Feed of what top traders buy; **copy trading**, leaderboard, live trade feed
- Chains: Solana, Base, BNB, Monad; later **Robinhood Chain**; perps via Hyperliquid (outside US)
- Fee quoted: **0.5%** on Solana spot, gas/priority paid by Fomo (promo site - verify in-app)
- Described as non-custodial (one source) - verify

## Numbers (medium confidence - mostly marketing/aggregator sites)
- $4B cumulative volume, 625k+ traders by June 2026
- Raised ~$19M by end of 2025 (Benchmark-led Series A, Nov 2025)
- Claimed biggest daily Solana terminal by volume from **6 Aug 2026**, ahead of Axiom
- Revenue 30 days to 22 Sep 2026: **$31.7M** vs Pump.fun $51.0M (Upshift)

## Risks / thinking points
- Frictionless buying = **frictionless losing**. Apple Pay -> impulse trades.
- Social feed = herd signal. Treat as data about *others' positions*, not advice. See [[Copy Trading and Following Wallets]].
- Many "reviews" are affiliate pieces with referral codes. Discount them. See [[Disclosure and Legal Risk]].
- Check it blocks/flags [[Bundling]]? Don't assume - run your own [[Bundle Detection Checklist]].
