# Who Actually Makes Money
Ranking by structural edge (high -> low):
1. **Platforms/terminals** - fees on every trade (Pump.fun ~$51M/30d, Fomo ~$31.7M/30d, Sept 2026)
2. **Bots/snipers** - speed + infra (one sniper bot reportedly made ~27k SOL in 30 days in Nov 2024)
3. **Serial deployers/insiders** - launches, creator fees, bundles (top deployers did 2,000+ tokens/month in Sept 2025, per Pisa study)
4. **KOLs** - paid posts ($50-$60k/post seen in the Sept 2025 ZachXBT leak), token allocations
5. **Disciplined manual traders** - small, uneven, mostly under $500/month. See [[Data Reality Check]]
6. **Late retail** - exit liquidity

## Takeaways
- You don't beat bots at speed. You beat *yourself*: sizing, exits, selectivity.
- "Screenshots of wins" = survivorship bias. Log everything in your own [[Trade Journal]].
- LP/infra/building plays exist but need skill and carry their own risk. See [[Content Ideas Bank]] (builder angle).
