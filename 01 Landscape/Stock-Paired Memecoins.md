# Stock-Paired Memecoins
Memecoin liquidity pools quoted against **Robinhood Stock Tokens** (tokenized equities 1:1 custodian-backed; *not* shares, no voting rights).

## Facts
- Daily volume in stock-token/memecoin pairs peaked **~$440M in early Sept 2026** (Bloomberg citing Dune/Adam Tehc), then **fell fast**. It is a *peak daily* figure - not a cumulative total as Grok's wording implies.
- 2 Sep 2026: $217M meme/stock-pair volume vs $127M direct stock-token trading that day.
- Sept research: 432 stock-paired pools, ~$8.84M in stock tokens, ~$95M 24h volume (thin liquidity).
- ~25% of Stock Token-related volume came from these pairs (Memeburn estimate).
- Spillover: small-cap **Farmmi (FAMI) jumped as much as 321% on 2 Sep** after Robinhood Chain traders piled into a mushroom memecoin + unofficial token. Real-world stock moves from on-chain hype.

## Why it's fragile
- Thin stock-token supply -> price premiums/discounts vs the real stock
- Feedback loop: meme hype inflates stock-token volume without real equity demand
- "Snapchat trade" feeling: you *need* the stock token to buy the meme
