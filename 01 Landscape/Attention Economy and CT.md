# Attention Economy and CT (Crypto Twitter)
X is discovery + narrative + price discovery. Grok claims >70% of retail finds tokens via X - **unverified**.

## Loop
1. Private TG/Discord alpha -> 2. KOL posts -> 3. CT quote-tweets/memes -> 4. terminals/trending -> 5. retail buys -> 6. insiders exit.
- If you learn of it at step 3-4, you're probably the liquidity. See [[Not Chasing Big Candles]].

## Mnemonic: "SEE" the cycle
- **S**eed (insiders/KOL)
- **E**cho (CT memes, engagement farming)
- **E**xit (distribution into the crowd)

## What actually moves a meme
- Simple, repeatable ticker + picture
- A hook people can post (joke, in-group, celeb/event)
- A distribution engine (KOLs, communities)
- Liquidity path to exit

## Counter-signals
- Engagement farming/bots: mid-tier crypto influencer accounts run ~**40-60% bot** (June 2026 analysis via rzlt). See [[Call Channels and Payouts]].
- Paid posts rarely disclosed. See [[Disclosure and Legal Risk]].

## Use it as a trader
- Follow ~10-20 accounts in a list; log who was early vs late. See [[Trade Journal]].
