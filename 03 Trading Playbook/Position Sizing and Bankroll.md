# Position Sizing and Bankroll
*Framework, not financial advice. I'm not a financial advisor.*

## Core rules
- **Bankroll = money you can lose entirely.** Not rent, tuition, debt, or client money.
- **Fixed unit**: each trade = 1-2% of bankroll (rule of thumb for lottery-style assets). Same unit regardless of how "sure" it feels.
- **Max open risk**: ~10% of bankroll across all open positions.
- **Daily loss limit**: ~5% of bankroll -> stop for the day. See [[Psychology and Tilt]].
- **Size down after losses, don't size up** (revenge sizing is how bankrolls die).
- Size creep after a win is the other trap. See [[The Big Win Protocol]].

## Why tiny
- Illiquid tokens: your stop may not execute (slippage). Small size = survivable.
- Win rate in this game is low; payoff skew must carry you. See [[Data Reality Check]].

## Math reminders
- Lose 50% -> need +100% to recover.
- 0.5%-fee round trip + slippage + priority fees eat small wins. Track *net* in [[Trade Journal]].

## Funding
- Fund only from surplus (e.g., a fixed % of monthly income). Never from borrowed money. See [[When You Run Out of Money]].
