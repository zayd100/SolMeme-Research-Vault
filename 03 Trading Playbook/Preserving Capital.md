# Preserving Capital
Rule #1: stay in the game.

## Checklist
- Fixed small unit ([[Position Sizing and Bankroll]])
- Daily loss limit + weekly review
- Profits swept to vault ([[The Big Win Protocol]])
- Separate wallets ([[Wallet Security and Scams]])
- No leverage/perps while learning
- Never hold the whole bankroll in one token
- Keep a "no-trade" list of tokens/callers/behaviors that burned you
- Prefer fewer, better trades. Fees compound against you.

## Habits
- Track *net* PnL after fees
- Review max drawdown monthly
- Take breaks. See [[Psychology and Tilt]]
