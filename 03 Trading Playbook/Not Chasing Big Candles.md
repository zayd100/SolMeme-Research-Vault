# Not Chasing Big Candles
If it's up 5x in minutes and you saw it on a timeline, you're probably the exit.

## Mnemonic: "WAIT-3"
1. **W**ait for a pullback (e.g., -30-50% off the high)
2. **A**ssess: higher low forming? volume holding?
3. **I**nvalidate: where is the thesis wrong? set it
4. **T**hen size small (half unit on re-entries after extension)

## Rules
- **Missed trade = free.** There's always another one tomorrow.
- No buys while a candle is vertical.
- No "big chunk" entries into thin liquidity. Slippage + dev dump = exit trap.
- Cap chase size: **half unit max**, and only after structure.
- If you feel urgency, that's the signal to log, not buy. See [[Psychology and Tilt]].

## Big chunk caution
- Large buy into a thin pool = you move the price against yourself, and you can't get out.
- Scale in with 2-3 smaller entries if the setup is real.
