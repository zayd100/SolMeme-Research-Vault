# Psychology and Tilt
## Tilt signals
- Revenge sizing, "just one more", skipping the checklist
- Tired, hungry, late-night, or distracted (exams/deadlines)
- Checking price constantly

## Controls
- Daily loss limit, max trades/day
- 10-minute rule before any entry
- No trading when exhausted or in a rush
- Screen-free breaks after a big win/loss
- Journal emotion per trade ([[Trade Journal]])

## Mindset
- Most outcomes are variance. Judge **process**, not result.
- Missed gains are free. See [[Not Chasing Big Candles]]
- Winning big can be as dangerous as losing. See [[The Big Win Protocol]]
