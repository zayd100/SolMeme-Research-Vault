# The Big Win Protocol
*For when a position hits something like 5x+, or one trade doubles your bankroll. Framework, not advice.*

## Mnemonic: **P.S.V.**
1. **P**rincipal out - sell enough to take your original stake off the table
2. **S**cale out - tiered sells ([[Exit Strategy and Scaling Out]])
3. **V**ault profits - move a fixed share OFF the trading wallet (stablecoin / bank / different wallet)

## Rules
- Decide the **sweep %** in advance (e.g., 50% of profit above bankroll target goes to the vault; vault is not for trading).
- **Unit size rises slowly** (e.g., +10-25% of unit per milestone), never "all in because I'm hot".
- **24h cooldown** after a big win before the next big position.
- Record the win and the *process* in [[Trade Journal]]. Was it skill or luck?
- Check local tax/reporting rules (varies by country). Keep records.

## Worked example (your 20 -> 350 run)
- At $350, banking even half = **$175 secured** vs $20 started.
- Remaining ~$175 can ride with a ladder. Worst case you're still +$155.
- Instead, 100% stayed exposed -> back to 0. See [[Case Study - 20 to 350 and Back]].

## Psychology
- Winning feels like skill. It's often variance. See [[Psychology and Tilt]].
