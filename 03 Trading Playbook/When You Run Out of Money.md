# When You Run Out of Money
**Do not "top up to win it back".**

## Protocol: **S.T.O.P.**
1. **S**top trading for 72h minimum
2. **T**rade autopsy - list last 20 trades: entry reason, size, exit, rule broken ([[Trade Journal]])
3. **O**nly surplus money later - no rent/tuition/credit/borrowed funds
4. **P**aper trade first ([[Paper Trading Method]]) until rules are followed ~90% of the time

## Rebuild options (low pressure)
- Build the bankroll slowly from surplus income, capped (e.g., a fixed % of monthly earnings)
- Learn/observe instead of trade: study trackers, journal calls, watch streams ([[Watch vs Trade - How To Learn]])
- Build tools/content around the scene ([[Content Ideas Bank]]) - skills compound, bankrolls don't

## Red flags (talk to someone)
- Trading to fix a money problem, hiding losses, chasing, sleep loss
- Borrowing to trade
- If it feels out of control, pause and talk to a trusted person or a support service in your country
