# Spotting Winners
**No formula. Mostly speed + insider access. Your edge is selectivity.**

## Mnemonic: "MOVE"
- **M**eme quality: simple, funny/relatable, easy to post, ticker people can say
- **O**rganic holders: growing steadily, not just a few big wallets; bundle/sniper clean ([[Bundle Detection Checklist]])
- **V**olume on dips: buyers show up after pullbacks (not only vertical candles)
- **E**arly-ish: you are not the 500th person to see the tweet

## Extra signals
- Real community content (people making memes, not just the dev)
- Smart/known wallets buying - but see [[Copy Trading and Following Wallets]]
- Narrative fits current attention (news, event, season). Check [[Attention Economy and CT]]
- Healthy liquidity vs market cap

## Skip signals
- Copycat of a trending name
- Only paid shills posting
- All the volume from a handful of wallets
- "Dev will do X soon" promises

## Practice
- Before trading, **rate 20 trending coins** on MOVE in your journal and see what happened after 24h. Paper-trade it. See [[Paper Trading Method]].
