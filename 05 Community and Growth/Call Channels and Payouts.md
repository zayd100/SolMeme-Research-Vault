# Call Channels and Payouts - worth it?
## How money flows
- Paid KOL posts ($50-$60k/post seen in ZachXBT's Sept 2025 leak of 160+ accepted payments)
- KOL "rounds": discounted token allocations ($1k-$20k typical) for posts
- Hybrid cash + vested tokens
- Referral/affiliate revenue (terminals, apps) - disclose ([[Disclosure and Legal Risk]])
- Paid group subscriptions

## Reality
- ~85% of paid KOL campaigns fail to show measurable ROI (Disence via rzlt)
- Mid-tier influencer bot rates ~40-60%
- <3% disclosed paid posts in that leak sample

## If you're **buying calls**
- Expected value is poor: you're often late and the group may front-run you
- Track each caller's *entry time vs your fill* and net result. See [[Copy Trading and Following Wallets]]

## If you're **becoming a caller**
- Payouts require audience + credibility. Early on = low or none.
- Conflicts of interest are everything. Calling something you hold or are paid for without saying so is the fastest way to lose trust.

## Verdict
- **Not worth it as a first goal.** Build credibility ([[Credibility Rules]]) and a record first. Paid promos later, disclosed, with limits.
