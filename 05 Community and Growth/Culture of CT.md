# Culture of CT (Crypto Twitter)
- "Trenches" = high-risk new-token trading; speed and memes rule
- Callers, KOLs, alpha groups, engagement farming, constant rotation
- In-jokes and tickers spread faster than facts
- Reputation = timestamps. Screenshots of wins are cheap; verifiable call history isn't.

## Why you have an edge
- You understand internet culture. Memes are a skill here. See [[Spotting Winners]]

## Pitfalls
- Everyone is "early". Check who's actually early ([[Attention Economy and CT]])
- Paid shills and fake engagement ([[Call Channels and Payouts]])
