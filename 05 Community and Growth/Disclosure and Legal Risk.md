# Disclosure and Legal Risk
*Not legal advice. Rules vary by country. Ask a qualified lawyer if you plan to earn from promotion.*

## Key points
- Paid promotion is legal; **hiding it isn't** (FTC-style endorsement guidance; EU MiCA adds crypto rules).
- Token allocations count like cash for disclosure.
- SEC charged Kim Kardashian over an undisclosed EthereumMax post: **$1.26M** (2022).
- Influencers can face risk even if they didn't write the contract, especially when claiming "guaranteed", "safe", "about to list", or hiding holdings.
- Pump-and-dump/manipulation risk applies to coordinated pushes.

## Practical rules
- Label paid posts (#ad / "paid") and say when you hold
- Don't promise returns. Don't hide insider allocations
- Don't coordinate buys
- Keep records of deals/wallets
