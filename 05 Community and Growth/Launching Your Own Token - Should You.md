# Launching Your Own Token - Should You?
**Default: not yet.**

## Why
- Conflict of interest the moment followers can buy your token
- Legal/disclosure exposure ([[Disclosure and Legal Risk]])
- Bundling/snipers/rug suspicions follow any creator ([[Bundling]])
- Creator fees tempt bad incentives (Pump.fun ~0.30% per Grok - unverified; see [[Pump.fun]])

## If you ever do
- Disclose all dev wallets and allocations; no hidden bundling
- No promises. Treat as a community experiment, not income
- Consider building audience + trust first ([[Building a Following]])
