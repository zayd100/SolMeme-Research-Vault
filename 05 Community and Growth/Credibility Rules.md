# Credibility Rules
- Timestamp every call; log wins **and** losses
- Disclose holdings and affiliations
- No guarantees, no "can't lose"
- Never call then dump on followers
- Correct mistakes publicly
- Teach process, not just tickers
- Verify claims before posting (CAs, partnerships)
