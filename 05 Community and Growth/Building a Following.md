# Building a Following (X first)
*Strategy framework. Platform mechanics change, so test.*

## Pick a lane (be credible, not loud)
- **Honest trench diary**: paper trades + real micro-trades with timestamps
- **Rug autopsies**: dissect bundles/rugs with screenshots ([[Bundle Detection Checklist]])
- **Tool builder**: dashboards/bots (builder angle)
- **Culture/meme commentary**: memes + context

## Steps
1. Profile: clear niche + disclosure line ("not financial advice, I may hold")
2. Post daily: one insight, one chart, one lesson
3. Threads weekly: deep dives ([[Content Ideas Bank]])
4. Engage with 10-20 accounts in your lane
5. Keep a **public, timestamped call log** (wins *and* losses). See [[Credibility Rules]]
6. Move engaged followers to Telegram/Discord later; don't start there

## Community growth
- Start small; value-first (alerts, checklists, post-mortems)
- Rules: no shilling, no guaranteed returns, no pump coordination
- Grow with consistency, not with bought followers (bot-rate warnings in [[Call Channels and Payouts]])

## Timeline
- Expect slow growth. Credibility compounds; hype doesn't.
