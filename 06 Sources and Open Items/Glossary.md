# Glossary
- **CT**: Crypto Twitter
- **KOL**: key opinion leader / influencer
- **CA**: contract address
- **Ape**: buy impulsively
- **Trenches**: new/high-risk token trading
- **Exit liquidity**: buyers who let insiders sell
- **Bundle**: many wallets buying in the same block ([[Bundling]])
- **Sniper**: fast buyer at launch ([[Snipers and Bots]])
- **Bonding curve**: price formula on launchpad ([[Pump.fun]])
- **Graduation**: migration from curve to AMM
- **Rug**: dev/insider dumping
- **Moonbag**: small remaining position after taking profits
- **Slippage**: price impact/execution difference
- **Priority fee**: extra fee to get included faster
- **PnL**: profit and loss
- **Unit**: fixed position size ([[Position Sizing and Bankroll]])
- **Drawdown**: peak-to-trough loss
