# Paper Trading Method
- Pick a "virtual bankroll" and fixed unit
- Log entry time/price from a chart *at the moment you decide*
- Use realistic slippage/fees (assume ~1-3% round trip on illiquid coins)
- Follow [[Pre-Trade Checklist]] + [[Exit Strategy and Scaling Out]]
- Record in [[Trade Journal]]
- Compare with callers' timing ([[Copy Trading and Following Wallets]])

## Limits
- Paper can't replicate fear or failed sells. That's why you go micro-live later.
