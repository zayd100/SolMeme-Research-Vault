# 30-Day Learning Plan
## Week 1: Observe
- Set up tools ([[Tools and Trackers]]), burner wallet ([[Wallet Security and Scams]])
- Watch trending feed daily; log 5 launches/day with MOVE scores ([[Spotting Winners]])
- Learn terms ([[Glossary]])

## Week 2: Paper
- [[Paper Trading Method]]: 20+ paper trades with checklist
- Track callers' timing and results

## Week 3: Micro-live
- Tiny fixed unit, max 3 trades/day
- Journal everything ([[Trade Journal]])

## Week 4: Review
- Weekly metrics: net PnL, win rate, avg win/loss, max drawdown
- Rule-adherence %: target >=90%
- Decide: scale, hold, or pause

## Scale-up gate
- 50+ trades, positive expectancy after fees, 90% rule adherence, no tilt events
