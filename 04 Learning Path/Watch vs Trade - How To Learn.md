# Watch vs Trade - How To Learn
**Short answer: both, in stages. Watch + paper trade first, then tiny real size.**

## Fortnite analogy
- Watching streamers teaches **meta + culture** (what's hot, how callers talk).
- It doesn't teach **execution under pressure** (sizing, exits, slippage, tilt).
- So: watch to learn patterns, trade tiny to learn feelings.

## Caution
- Streamers/callers often hold bags or are paid. Check disclosure. ([[Disclosure and Legal Risk]])
- Don't copy their size. Their bankroll isn't yours.

## Plan
1. Observe 1-2 weeks, log calls, build glossary ([[Glossary]])
2. Paper trade with rules ([[Paper Trading Method]])
3. Micro-live trades with fixed unit ([[Position Sizing and Bankroll]])
4. Scale only after proof ([[30-Day Learning Plan]])
